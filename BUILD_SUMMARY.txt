DATABASE SCHEMA
===============

Collections:
  1. users - Authentication & profiles
  2. families - Multi-user groups
  3. expenses - Transaction records with splits
  4. categories - Expense categories
  5. budgets - Spending limits & tracking
  6. transactions - Settlement records (who owes whom)

Indexes:
  • User: email, familyIds
  • Family: ownerId, members.userId
  • Expense: familyId, date, category, paidBy
  • Category: familyId
  • Budget: familyId
  • Transaction: familyId, fromUser, toUser
